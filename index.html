The obstacle

Growth stalls when leads, follow-up and admin all rely on you.

Referrals worked when the business was small. Now enquiries come from more places, follow-up depends on memory, and hiring a marketing coordinator, a follow-up assistant and an admin person feels like three salaries too many.

Marketing

Inconsistent leads

  • No time to manage Meta/Facebook Ads properly
  • Over-reliance on referrals and word of mouth
  • No repeatable way to create enquiries on demand

Follow-up

Leads go quiet

  • Slow response, or one call and then silence
  • Quotes and old leads are rarely chased twice
  • No one knows which follow-up got missed

Admin

The owner is the system

  • Onboarding, reminders and paperwork eat the week
  • Tasks fall through when they're not written down
  • No single view of what actually needs attention

Is this you?
